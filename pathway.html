Steps to Become a Pilot

Becoming a pilot is a structured journey that blends education, aviation training, and hands-on experience. This page breaks down each step from early interest to earning your commercial rating.

1. Explore the Career

Students begin by learning about different pilot roles, required ratings, lifestyle, salary expectations, and career paths such as regional airlines, major airlines, or military aviation.

2. Begin Flight Training

Training starts with earning a Private Pilot License (PPL), followed by the Instrument Rating and Commercial License. Students learn navigation, safety, communication, and technical aircraft systems.

3. Build Flight Hours

Pilots gain experience through instructor roles, time-building programs, or flying clubs. Most major airlines require around 1,500 total flight hours.

4. Apply to Airline Pathway Programs

Airline-sponsored programs provide mentorship, structured training, and a clear route to the flight deck. United offers one of the most supportive pathways in the industry.
