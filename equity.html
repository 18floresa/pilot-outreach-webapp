Support for Underrepresented Students

Many students face barriers related to cost, access, or lack of industry exposure. This page highlights programs designed to support first-generation, low-income, and underrepresented students who want to pursue aviation.

Financial Support

Scholarships, grants, and airline-sponsored training assistance can dramatically lower the cost of pilot training. United and its partners offer financial pathways to help students begin without heavy upfront expenses.

Mentorship & Community

Students gain access to professional pilots, aviation mentors, and support networks that guide them through training, job applications, and industry expectations.

Exposure to Aviation Careers

Programs host tours, workshops, simulator experiences, and classroom visits so students can understand what a pilot career truly looks like and feel confident taking the next step.
