About This Project

This site is a student-built prototype designed to help improve the visibility of aviation career pathways for youth, especially those without direct access to the aviation industry.

Our Mission

The goal is to provide clear information, supportive resources, and a simple roadmap for anyone interested in becoming a pilot. Accessibility, clarity, and opportunity are the foundation of this project.

Why Pilot Pathways Matter

The aviation industry is facing a long-term pilot shortage. Expanding access helps strengthen the future workforce while giving students meaningful, well-paid career opportunities in a growing field.

Thank You

Thank you for exploring this prototype. Your feedback helps shape the design and direction of future improvements.
